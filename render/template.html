1USA · China · Tech & law

US jury convicts Huawei of trade-secret theft and fraud

A federal jury in Brooklyn finds China's Huawei guilty of conspiring to steal trade secrets, bank and wire fraud and breaking Iran sanctions, after an 11-week trial. Huawei says it will appeal.

2Saudi Arabia · Yemen's Houthis

Riyadh's tallest tower hit again; 11 injured

Saudi civil defence blames a Houthi "terrorist attack" on the PIF Tower, home of the kingdom's wealth fund, struck for the second time in five days. Gulf neighbours condemn it.

3China · Politics

China investigates Shanghai party chief Chen Jining

The anti-graft watchdog is investigating the Politburo member for "serious violations of discipline and law"; he is the fourth Politburo member probed since 2025.

4Markets · AI

AI stocks slip: Nvidia −5.9%, Nasdaq −1.5%

Nvidia gives back part of Monday's 31.5% jump on reinstating CEO Jensen Huang; newly listed Anthropic sinks below its $83 IPO price. Dow +0.9%.

5Business · Pharma

Sanofi agrees to buy Insmed for about $41 billion

The French drugmaker pays $175 a share in cash for the US lung-disease specialist.

6Gaza · Israel

Hamas says Israeli strike killed its Gaza security chief

Medics say six others died, two of them police. Israel says it struck a senior Hamas operative.

7Ukraine · War

Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia flats kills 2, wounds 40

Four children are among the wounded in the overnight attack, says governor Mykhailo Semikin.

8Europe · Migration

European rights court faults Poland, Latvia over pushbacks

Returns to Belarus amid migration it engineered are not unlawful per se, but expelling children and a protected applicant was. Lithuania cleared.

9Africa · Football

Court hands Senegal back the Africa Cup of Nations title

CAS overturns Morocco's forfeit win; Senegal's 1-0 final victory stands. Morocco is "astonished".

10Space · Science

Euclid finds the most distant quasar yet

ESA's telescope sees it as it was just 630 million years after the Big Bang, lit by a black hole of ~360 million Suns.

11Health · DR Congo

Gavi plans to buy 1.5m Ebola vaccine doses for Congo

The order for the experimental shot hinges on trials; the outbreak has killed over 5,700 (Reuters).
