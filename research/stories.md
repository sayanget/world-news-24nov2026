## Leads

1. **Huawei convicted in US criminal trial.** A federal jury in Brooklyn found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts, including conspiracy to steal trade secrets, bank fraud, wire fraud and Iran-sanctions violations. It acquitted the company of racketeering conspiracy (the central charge) and conspiracy to obstruct justice. The trial ran about 11 weeks, with about 4 days of deliberation. Sentencing is due next September; Huawei says it will appeal and that the acquittal "vindicates" its position.

2. **Riyadh's PIF Tower hit again; 11 injured.** Saudi Civil Defense says 11 people were injured (2 Saudis, 2 Indians, 3 Nepalis, 4 Egyptians) in what it called a Houthi "terrorist attack" on the financial-district tower. Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki called it "cowardly". It is the second strike on the tower in five days (first on 19 Nov, no injuries). Reports differ on drone vs missile. UAE, GCC and the Muslim World League condemned it.
   - https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/saudi-led-coalition-condemns-terrorist-attack-on-tower-in-riyadh
   - https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/11-injured-in-houthi-attack-on-riyadh-s-financial-district-tower-saudi-civil-defense/4136093
   - https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/riyadhs-landmark-pif-tower-struck-aerial-attack-second-time-five-days
   - Context, same day: Iran's army chief said Hormuz stays closed to unauthorised traffic until Iran's seven conditions are met (CBS/CNBC, 24 Nov). The IRGC's claim that 3 tankers hit mines was made late on 23 Nov and JMIC calls it unconfirmed, so it is treated as background only.
3. **China investigates Shanghai party chief Chen Jining.** The CCDI is investigating the Politburo member, 62, for "serious violations of discipline and law" (CCTV/Xinhua). Reuters counts him as the fourth Politburo member probed since 2025. Shanghai's leadership held a late-night meeting pledging loyalty to Beijing.
